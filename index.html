FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take walk-in clients or is it by appointment only?

I primarily work by appointment to ensure adequate time for custom design and consultation. However, I may accommodate walk-in consultations when my schedule allows. For the best experience and guaranteed availability, I recommend booking in advance.

How long does it take to get a custom tattoo design?

Design timelines vary based on complexity and scheduling. After our initial consultation, I typically provide custom designs within 1-2 weeks. Larger or more intricate pieces may require additional design time to ensure the artwork meets your expectations.

What tattoo styles do you specialize in?

I specialize in black & grey tattoos, with a focus on dark fantasy themes and organic design techniques. My work emphasizes body flow, composition, and longevity. Each piece is custom-designed to complement your individual anatomy and personal style.

How much does a custom tattoo cost in Tel Aviv?

Pricing depends on size, placement, detail level, and time required. I discuss specific pricing during consultations once I understand your design concept. This ensures accurate quotes based on the actual scope of your custom tattoo project.

Do you tattoo in English/for tourists visiting Tel Aviv?

Yes, I tattoo clients from all over the world and speak English fluently. Many of my clients are tourists visiting Tel Aviv. I'm happy to accommodate international visitors and can help coordinate scheduling around your travel plans.
